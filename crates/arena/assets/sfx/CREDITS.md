# Sound credits

The one-shot sound effects (`<name>_<n>.ogg`) are built by `../tools/build_sfx.py` from these
recordings, all released under CC0 (public domain):

- Kenney (kenney.nl): Casino Audio, Interface Sounds, Impact Sounds, RPG Audio, UI Audio, Music Jingles
- rubberduck (opengameart.org): 80 CC0 RPG SFX

The ambient loops (`ambient.ogg`, `ambient_match.ogg`) are synthesised by `../tools/gen_sfx.py`.
